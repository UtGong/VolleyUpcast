The app only illustrates source 1.50–2.70s, using rally time = source time - 1.50. Two anonymous players are modeled. Other players and subsequent rally events are not reconstructed.
Visible source frames inspected manually; approximate joint points have ±15px marking tolerance at 1920x1080 inspection scale. Vertical-plane arm fitting is an explicit, unmeasured depth assumption. No automatic tracking or 3D estimator ran.
Approximate attack overlap source 2.0167s ±0.05s; possible blocker overlap 2.0833s ±0.05s. Exact impact/deflection is uncertain. Court position, height, body proportions and jump lift remain unconfirmed illustrative values.
